What to watch for

  • A sudden jump in the block rate usually means a scan, an attack, or a rule that was changed by mistake.
  • One source blocked on many different ports in a short time is a port scan. The alert panel flags this.
  • Blocked ports 22 (SSH), 23 (Telnet), and 3389 (remote desktop) show someone probing for remote access.
  • A rule with zero decisions may be dead or unnecessary. A rule with far more than the others deserves a look.
  • Check the “Why” column. Every block names the rule that caused it, or the default policy.
